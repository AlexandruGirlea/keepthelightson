# RFC 0000: Title

## Summary

*One paragraph. What changes, in plain words, for someone who has read the specification once.*

## Motivation

*What operational failure does this prevent, or what real problem does it solve? Name the
capability, the sector or the incident. Say what happens today if nothing changes.*

*Where possible, include a dated incident or implementation result.*

## Specification

*The normative text, written so it could be pasted into `spec/SPECIFICATION.md` with only the
clause numbers adjusted.*

*This is the only section of an RFC where BCP 14 keywords in capitals belong. Use MUST, MUST NOT,
SHOULD, SHOULD NOT and MAY as defined in section 2 of the specification, and use them precisely. If
you cannot decide between MUST and SHOULD, that indecision is the substance of the proposal and
belongs in Unresolved questions.*

*Give the clause numbers you are adding, changing or removing. Quote the current text of anything
you are changing so a reviewer can see both versions without leaving the page.*

## Rationale and alternatives

*Why this design and not another. What else was considered, including the option of doing nothing
and the option of solving it outside the specification, in guidance or in an example.*

*If a clause is a number (90 days, two people, 120 days), justify the number. Include supporting evidence.*

## Drawbacks

*Why should this not be done? Cost to implementers who already conform. Ways it could be gamed.
Sectors it fits badly. Text it makes longer or harder to read.*

*Include the strongest objection to the proposal and explain how it affects the decision.*

## Impact on existing conformance claims

*The section that decides the version number. Answer directly:*

- *Would an organisation that has published a declaration against the current version still be
  making an accurate statement after this change?*
- *If not, what exactly would they have to redo: a re-drill, a re-measurement, a new register
  entry, a corrected published declaration?*
- *Which semantic version bump does this imply under section 7 of `GOVERNANCE.md`, and therefore
  which Final Comment Period length applies under section 5.4?*

*"None" is a perfectly good answer. Say why.*

## How to teach this

*Explain the change to the people doing the work. Include a short paragraph suitable for an
internal guide.*

## Prior art

*Where has this been done before, and how did it go? Other standards, other regulatory regimes,
other industries. Aviation, medicine, power generation and finance have all been solving versions
of this for decades.*

*Include failures of similar approaches.*

## Unresolved questions

*What must be settled before this RFC can be accepted, and what can reasonably be left to a later
proposal? Be explicit about which is which.*

*List the questions reviewers need to resolve.*
